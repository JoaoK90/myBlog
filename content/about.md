Hi, I'm João Kennedy, a Brazilian student now based in Évry, near Paris.

I'm from a small city in the state of São Paulo. I'm pursuing a double diploma in Digital Engineering at Télécom SudParis, thanks to a France Excellence Eiffel scholarship. My french graduation track focuses on computer vision and AI, connected to the Virtual & Augmented Reality programme at Institut Polytechnique de Paris.

This blog is where I'll share my homework and projects from the Virtual & Augmented Reality (VAR) Master: what I built, what I learned, and what went wrong along the way. I hope it can be a good record of my own progress.

Outside of class, I enjoy hiking, science fiction, piano, traveling and photographing.

Thanks for stopping by, and feel free to reach me out through my contacts!
